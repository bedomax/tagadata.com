Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 19, 2026

tagadata is a news aggregator. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data from our users.

Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or any other identifying information. There are no user accounts, no sign-up, and no login. There are no contact forms that store any information.

Local Storage

The app saves only your country preference (e.g. "cl" or "ec") in your browser's localStorage to remember your selection between visits. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to our servers.

Country Detection

On your first visit, we make a request to an IP geolocation service (ip-api.com) solely to suggest the most relevant country. We do not log or store your IP address in our systems.

Cookies

We do not use cookies of any kind — neither first-party nor third-party.

Third-Party Services

tagadata displays headlines linked to external news outlets. When you click an article, you are taken to that outlet's website, which has its own privacy policy independent of tagadata.

YouTube videos are loaded on demand (only when you click play). YouTube may set its own cookies when a video is played.

Analytics and Advertising

We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or any tracking or advertising tools.

Children's Privacy

tagadata does not collect data from any user, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever introduce any data collection practices in the future, we will update this page and communicate the changes clearly.
